Stable Kingdoms · Game setup

Set up your kingdoms

Choose the kingdoms in play and how many seats are controlled by humans. All remaining seats will be controlled by random bots. The first player is randomly determined each new game. Deck sizes: 1 kingdom 80 cards total; 2: 70; 3: 60; 4: 50; 5: 45; 6: 42; 7: 40; 8: 38 cards per kingdom.

Choose each kingdom's culture. Random chooses an available culture; specific choices may repeat. Online multiplayer supports these culture choices.

Welsh queens and Continental Germanic kings use uncrowned stand-ins until crown artwork arrives. Lombard / Italian, Visigoth / Iberian, and Frankish each have 160 portraits, crowned and uncrowned. Portraits do not change gameplay. Characters' art does not change their statistics or game rules.
